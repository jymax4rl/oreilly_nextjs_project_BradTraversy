# Guest Policies — Isisel

> **Template for product use — not formal legal advice.**  
> **Version:** `kama-terms-v1.0-2026-08-31`

## Booking

Provide accurate guest details, respect house rules, and treat the property and neighbors with care. You are responsible for your party.

## Cancellations

Follow the listing booking policy and confirmed written agreements. Use in-product modify/cancel tools when available.

## Payments

MVP may use cash, mobile money, or other off-platform methods agreed with the host. Prefer traceable references. Never share OTPs or passwords.

## Disputes

Message the host first with facts. Contact Isisel with evidence if unresolved. Informal mediation only in MVP — not a court.

## Safety

Leave if you feel unsafe and contact local emergency services when needed. Report trust & safety issues to Isisel.
