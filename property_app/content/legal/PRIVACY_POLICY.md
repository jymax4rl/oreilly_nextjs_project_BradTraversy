## What we collect

Account data (name, email, profile image via Google sign-in), listings, bookings, messages, and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

## How we use data

Operate bookings and messaging, moderate content, support users, prevent fraud, and improve the product. We do not sell personal data.

## Processors

Infrastructure providers (database, auth, email, image CDN, analytics) process data to deliver the service under our instructions.

## Your choices

Update profile details in Settings. For deletion or broader requests, contact us via published channels. Clearing browser storage may remove local preferences (language, terms acceptance).

## Cookies

Essential storage for session, security, and preferences. Privacy-conscious analytics may be used. See Policies → Cookies.
