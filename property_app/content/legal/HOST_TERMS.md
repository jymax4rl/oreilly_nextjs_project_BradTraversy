# Host Terms — Kama Properties

> **Template for product use — not formal legal advice.**

> **Version:** `kama-terms-v1.0-2026-08-31` · **Last updated:** 31 August 2026

## 1. Listing accuracy

Photos, amenities, location context, capacity, pricing, and house rules must fairly represent the stay. Misleading listings may be unpublished or suspended.

## 2. Availability calendar honesty

Keep calendars current. Do not accept bookings you cannot honor. Repeated double-booking or guest ghosting may lead to enforcement.

## 3. Payments (MVP)

Payments may be arranged off-platform (cash, mobile money, bank transfer) by agreement with guests. You must still honor confirmed stays and communicate payment expectations clearly before arrival. Kama does not hold those funds in MVP.

## 4. Moderation & suspension

We may moderate content, restrict features, or suspend accounts for fraud, safety risk, discrimination, illegal activity, or repeated harm to guests.

## 5. Intellectual property

You keep rights in your photos and listing text and grant Kama a license to display them. You must have rights to everything you upload. Kama brand and software remain ours.

## 6. Liability limits

Stay-related liability is primarily between host and guest. Platform liability is limited as described in the Terms of Service. Obtain local insurance and licenses as required in your jurisdiction — Kama is not your insurer or licensing authority.

## 7. Market context (Gambia / Africa)

We build for African hospitality realities, including mobile money and relationship-driven bookings. Local lodging, tax, and consumer rules still apply to you as host.

## 8. Future land marketplace

The platform may expand into land buy/sell. Listing land will require separate seller terms, identity checks, and compliance steps when launched. Using rental hosting today does not entitle you to sell land on Kama.

## 9. Contact

Trust & safety and host policy questions: use published contact channels on the live site.
