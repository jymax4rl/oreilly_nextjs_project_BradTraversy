Isisel Creators · 04

Make influencers work for a specific apartment

The promoter’s growth product: turn creators into paid partners for one listing at a time — with rules you control from your host console.

01

Add a creator

Name, platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube…), profile URL. Invite them with a portal link.

02

Assign a stay

Attach them to one apartment with a unique promo code guests can enter when they book.

Set the money

04

Pause anytime

Pause a code or a partnership when the push is done. Attribution stops; you stay in charge.

✓Commission you fund — paid on attributed accommodation when their code converts. Separate from any Isisel platform fee.

✓Promoter visibility — see active codes, attributed stays, booking value, and commissions from Host → Creators.

Isisel The founding reason · 05

Mobile Money is why Isisel exists

Global OTAs assume a foreign card. Most African travelers already pay with Mobile Money. Bridging that gap is the door to an intra-African short-term rental economy.

The problem

Demand is local. Rails were not.

A guest in Abidjan or Accra who can pay Wave or MoMo should not be locked out of a Dakar apartment because the only checkout is a foreign card network.

The build

GeniusPay on Isisel

Payment integration for African Mobile Money (Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo, Moov) and cards — so settlement can follow how the continent already moves money.

The effect

Intra-African circulation

More African guests can book African stays. More nights fill. Promoters unlock a market that international platforms under-serve.

The present

Human first, rails ready

Guests request stays and reach you on WhatsApp today. Online MoMo/card rails are built into the platform so checkout can open as you scale — without rebuilding payments later.

Close the pitch here: “Creators fill the feed. Mobile Money fills the wallet gap. Together they let African stays grow with African money.”
