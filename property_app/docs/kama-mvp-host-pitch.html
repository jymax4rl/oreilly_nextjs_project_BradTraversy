Zero commission for founding hosts

You set the rate. You and the guest agree how to pay. We take no cut on founding-host listings while the marketplace grows.

02 · Control

Requests, not missed calls

Guests ask for dates in the app. You answer when you cook, drive, or sleep. Nothing books itself behind your back.

Calendar, requests, and messages live together. WhatsApp stays for hospitality — not for hunting who asked what, when.

05 · Timing

Founding hosts are not waiting for demand to arrive. They are the catalog. Less competition for attention in your city. A direct relationship with the people building the platform. Your feedback shapes host tools as we grow.
