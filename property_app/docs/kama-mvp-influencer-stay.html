Isisel Terms · 03

What this is — and is not

Clear terms keep the partnership honest. Read these before you introduce anyone.

Stay

Two nights, not cash

The offer is two nights of accommodation with a participating host. It is not transferable for money, flights, or a fee.

Condition

The host must join

The stay is unlocked when the owner you introduce completes onboarding and is approved. An introduction alone is not enough.

Dates

Calendar first

Nights follow the host’s availability. We coordinate with you; we do not override a calendar that is already full.

Content

No mandatory post

We are not buying a reel. If you share the stay, tag isisel.com. If you do not, the stay still stands once the host is live.

One offer

One partnership unless we agree otherwise

Standard offer is one two-night stay per creator. Larger introductions can be discussed in writing.

Conduct

Guest rules still apply

House rules, occupancy, and respect for the home are required. We may decline a stay that cannot be hosted safely.
