Section 02

Executive Summary

Isisel is a live African vacation-rentals marketplace. Creator partnerships are the near-term demand and trust engine: influencers bring audiences; hosts pay a creator share only when a qualifying booking completes.

Governing principle

Creator commission ≠ Isisel platform commission. Isisel does not charge hosts a standard platform commission for this creator marketing model. Hosts only share a percentage when they voluntarily opt in and the creator generates a qualifying booking. Creator pay is a marketing expense on a dedicated ledger — never reuse pricingSnapshot.platformFee.

Section 10

Commission Flow (Post-Stay)

Booking.confirmed + attribution (creatorId, code) + checkOut < today + status ≠ cancelled │ ▼ Commission Engine evaluates rules + fraud gates │ ▼ CreatorCommission status: accrued → approved → payable → paid │ ▼ Payout batch (ops) → creator receives funds
Governing principle

Finalize after stay (checkout date past). Do not pay on pending. Cancelled or unlisted stays that never complete do not create payable commissions.

Section 19

Commission Engine

  • Inputs: booking snapshot (accommodation base, nights, currency), campaign rate, attribution, stay completion, fraud flags.
  • Output: one CreatorCommission row per qualifying booking (idempotent on bookingId).
  • Formula (default): amount = accommodationBase × creatorRate (e.g. 10%).
  • Non-goals: changing guest total solely to fund creator; writing creator amounts into platformFee.
  • Reversals: if booking later cancelled post-accrual but pre-pay, mark reversed; after pay, ops clawback process.
Governing principle

Creator commission ≠ Isisel platform commission. The engine may read pricingSnapshot for bases; it must write only to CreatorCommission.

Section 20

Fraud Prevention

Attribution integrity
  • Code required at checkout (or signed deep link)
  • Reject self-booking (creator = guest)
  • Velocity caps per code / IP / device
  • Host–creator collusion review queue
Payout gates
  • Pay only post-checkout + confirmed
  • Manual approve above threshold
  • Hold on dispute / chargeback
  • Audit log on status transitions

Section 26

Legal / Commercial Considerations

  • Written agreement per creator/campaign: rate, scope, content expectations, payout schedule, termination.
  • IP & brand: existing legal content covers creator materials; commercial revenue share only via agreement.
  • Tax / KYC: collect payout identity where required; creators responsible for their tax status.
  • Host consent: campaign terms disclosed before attributed bookings bind economics.
  • Disputes: chargebacks freeze payable rows; ops playbook for reversals.
  • No exclusivity by default unless negotiated.

Section 30

Conclusion

Partner with creators. Protect the fee. Pay on performance.

Isisel already has the public funnel and ops CRM. This system adds host opt-in, attribution, a CreatorCommission ledger, and clear economics so influencers earn when they deliver stays — without confusing creator pay with platform commission.

Governing principle — final reminder

Creator commission ≠ Isisel platform commission. Isisel does not charge hosts a standard platform commission for this creator marketing model. Hosts only share when they opt in and a creator generates a qualifying booking. Finalize after stay. Never reuse platformFee for creator pay.
