Isisel Why join early · 02

Why join early?

Early hosts are not waiting for the marketplace to arrive. They are helping to build it — with visibility, recognition, and a direct relationship with the platform.

01 · Visibility

Early visibility

Your property gets the opportunity to be among the first listings travelers discover as Isisel grows. Being early means less competition for attention in your destination.

02 · Recognition

Early-Bird recognition

Your property can receive an Early-Bird Host badge, helping distinguish you from properties that join later. Show travelers that you were here from the beginning.

03 · Advantages

Early-Bird advantages

As an early host, you may receive preferential benefits as the platform develops, including:

  • Early access to new host features
  • Priority consideration for promotional campaigns
  • Opportunities to participate in future platform initiatives
  • Early access to new markets and services
  • Special conditions reserved for founding hosts
04 · Promotion

Help us promote your property

As Isisel grows, selected properties may be featured through:

  • Social media campaigns
  • Destination guides
  • Featured-property collections
  • Marketing campaigns & editorial content
  • Partner promotions
05 · Partnership

Direct relationship with the platform

Early hosts aren’t just users. You help shape Isisel. Your feedback can influence future features, host tools, booking systems and services.
