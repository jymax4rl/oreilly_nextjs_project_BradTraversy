Dear PADDCO Team,

I hope this message finds you well.

Zanzibar is becoming one of Africa’s most important destinations for tourism, investment and real estate — and PADDCO is already building the places where the next generation of residents, investors and visitors will live and stay.

We are writing to propose a strategic technology and distribution partnership between PADDCO and Isisel.

Isisel is an Africa-focused property marketplace and property-management platform. Our aim is simple: you build the properties; we help build the digital infrastructure around them — distribution, reservations, operations, cleaning coordination and more.

For the initial phase, we are prepared to provide access to relevant Isisel capabilities at no upfront cost, so PADDCO can evaluate the platform before any long-term commercial commitment.

You will find the full proposal in the attached PDF, including:

• What PADDCO receives (marketplace distribution, property management tools, Isisel Cleaners, data visibility)
• Why we are approaching this as a partnership rather than a conventional software sale
• What we would ask from PADDCO in return
• A proposed five-phase pilot

We would welcome the opportunity to meet your team, walk through Isisel, and discuss a limited pilot for selected PADDCO properties.
