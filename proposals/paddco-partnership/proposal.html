The Platform

What is Isisel?

Isisel is an Africa-focused property marketplace and property-management platform designed to connect:

Properties → Hosts → Guests → Operations

Our objective is to build the digital infrastructure that allows African properties to be discovered, managed and operated more efficiently.

Isisel combines property distribution with operational tools for hosts, including reservations, property management, cleaning operations and other services required to run hospitality properties.

We are building Isisel with Africa’s realities in mind — including mobile-first users, local payment ecosystems, multilingual markets and the operational challenges that traditional international platforms often do not solve particularly well.

Value

What PADDCO gets

01 — Digital Property Distribution

An additional channel without building one

PADDCO properties can be presented through the Isisel marketplace. Potential guests and users can discover:

  • Apartments
  • Villas
  • Residences
  • Holiday properties
  • Investment properties
  • Other eligible PADDCO developments

Each property can have its own digital profile containing professional photographs, property description, amenities, location, availability, pricing, check-in/check-out information, maps and property-specific information.

This gives PADDCO an additional digital distribution channel without having to build one internally.

02 — Property Management Infrastructure

Technology that disappears behind the operation

For properties intended for short-term or hospitality use, Isisel can provide operational tools allowing PADDCO or its property managers to manage:

  • Reservations
  • Availability
  • Guests
  • Property information
  • Check-in / check-out
  • Cleaning
  • Operational tasks
  • Reservation history

The objective is to make the technology disappear behind the operation. PADDCO focuses on the real estate. Isisel handles the digital infrastructure.

03 — Isisel Cleaners

Operational readiness guests will notice

One of the operational modules we are developing is Isisel Cleaners. Hosts and property managers will be able to:

  • Add cleaners
  • Assign cleaners to properties
  • Automatically generate cleaning requests after checkout
  • Schedule cleaning jobs
  • Manage cleaning calendars
  • Receive completion reports
  • Receive photographs
  • Receive audio reports
  • Review cleaners

This is particularly important in hospitality. A guest may never see the property-management software. But they will absolutely notice whether the property was properly prepared.

04 — Audio-Based Reporting

Designed for African operational realities

We are designing Isisel with accessibility and African operational realities in mind. Cleaners will be able to report issues through voice recordings, photographs and optional written notes.

“The bathroom sink is leaking and there is a crack on the bedroom mirror.”

Example cleaner audio report

The property manager can receive the audio report together with photographs. This removes the requirement for cleaners to produce lengthy written reports and creates a simple operational communication system.

05 — A Future PADDCO Digital Ecosystem

Beyond listings — one operational layer

The partnership can eventually go beyond property listings. Imagine PADDCO having a digital operational layer connecting:

PADDCO → Properties → Reservations → Guests → Cleaners → Maintenance → Property Managers → Analytics

All through one ecosystem. This is where we believe Isisel can become significantly more valuable than a conventional property listing platform.

06 — Data & Operational Visibility

A clearer picture of portfolio performance

As the platform develops, PADDCO could have access to operational insights such as:

  • Property performance
  • Reservation activity
  • Occupancy
  • Cleaning activity
  • Guest activity
  • Property demand
  • Popular locations
  • Booking trends
  • Operational issues

The objective is to give management a clearer picture of how the portfolio is performing.
